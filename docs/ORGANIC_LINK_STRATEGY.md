# Organic link strategy

Earn links through genuinely useful assets: free calculators, original aggregated statistics when available, future embeddable calculators, developer resources, business templates, educational references, and outreach to relevant publishers. Product Hunt, reputable directories, and startup listings are optional distribution channels, not substitutes for utility.

Do not buy link packages, automate comments, use PBNs, mass-produce guest posts, or create fake reviews. An embed strategy may be evaluated later; if approved it must include explicit attribution, CSP/security boundaries, and a documented removal path.
