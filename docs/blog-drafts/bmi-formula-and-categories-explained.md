# BMI Formula and BMI Categories Explained

Body mass index (BMI) is a screening measure based on weight and height. It is not a diagnosis or a complete measure of health.

## Formula and categories

Metric BMI is `weight in kilograms / height in metres²`. Imperial BMI is `703 × weight in pounds / height in inches²`. For adults, common screening ranges are underweight below 18.5, healthy range 18.5–24.9, overweight 25.0–29.9, and obesity 30.0 or higher.

## Example

An adult weighing 70 kg and measuring 1.75 m has a BMI of about 22.9. Try the [BMI calculator](/calculator/bmi-calculator) with the correct unit system.

## Limitations

Adult BMI categories do not account for muscle mass, pregnancy, age-related body composition, ethnicity-specific guidance, or where fat is carried. Children use age- and sex-specific growth references. Speak with a qualified clinician for personal advice.
