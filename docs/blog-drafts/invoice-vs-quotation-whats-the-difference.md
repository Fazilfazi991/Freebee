# Invoice vs Quotation: What’s the Difference?

A quotation proposes scope and price before work is accepted. An invoice requests payment for goods or services that have been supplied or become payable.

## What belongs in each document?

A quotation normally includes scope, assumptions, quantities, proposed prices, validity, taxes, and acceptance terms. An invoice adds an invoice number, issue date, payment due date, the delivered goods or services, amount due, and payment instructions.

## A simple workflow

Describe the proposed work in a [quotation](/quotation-generator), confirm acceptance, deliver the work, then issue an [invoice](/invoice-generator) for the agreed amount. Keep the quotation and invoice references together.

## Important caveat

Tax invoices, numbering, payment terms, and required fields vary by jurisdiction and business type. Review the final document against local rules and your accounting records.
