# How to Calculate Loan Payments and Total Interest

An amortizing loan payment covers interest and principal. As the balance falls, the interest portion usually falls too.

## The payment formula

For a fixed-rate loan, the periodic payment is `M = P[i(1+i)^n]/[(1+i)^n-1]`, where `P` is principal, `i` is the periodic interest rate, and `n` is the number of payments. Total interest is total payments minus principal.

## Example

A $10,000 loan at 6% annual interest over three years has a monthly rate of 0.5% and 36 payments. The exact payment depends on lender rounding; use the [loan calculator](/calculator/loan-calculator) and inspect the [amortization calculator](/calculator/amortization-calculator) for the schedule.

## What the estimate does not include

Origination fees, insurance, taxes, late charges, variable rates, and lender-specific rounding can change the amount actually paid. Confirm the disclosure from the lender before signing.
