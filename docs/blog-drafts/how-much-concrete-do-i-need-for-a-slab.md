# How Much Concrete Do I Need for a Slab?

Estimate slab concrete by multiplying length × width × thickness, then convert the volume to the unit used by your supplier.

## Example

A 10 ft × 12 ft slab at 4 in thick is 40 cubic feet before waste. Divide by 27 for about 1.48 cubic yards, then add a practical allowance for uneven excavation and spillage. The [concrete calculator](/calculator/concrete-calculator) can handle the unit conversion.

## Before ordering

Measure several points when the ground is uneven, include thickened edges or footings separately, and ask the supplier how much allowance they recommend. Reinforcement and structural design are separate decisions.

## Limitations

This is a quantity estimate, not a structural specification. Local code, soil, drainage, load, mix design, access, and placement conditions may require a contractor or engineer.
