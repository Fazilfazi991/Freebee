# Legal release checklist

Generated drafts are not legal advice. Obtain qualified review for launch jurisdictions.

- [ ] Legal entity
- [ ] Consumer-facing company/product relationship
- [ ] Jurisdiction and governing law
- [ ] Registered address, if required
- [ ] General contact email
- [ ] Privacy contact
- [ ] Cookie contact
- [ ] Policy effective date
- [ ] Privacy policy approval
- [ ] Terms approval
- [ ] Cookie notice and consent-law review
- [ ] Data retention and deletion position for any future server feature
- [ ] Analytics/advertising disclosure before either is enabled
- [ ] Electronic-signature disclaimer approval

Populate the centralized `legal` fields in `app/config/platform.ts`; do not scatter identity values through route copy.
